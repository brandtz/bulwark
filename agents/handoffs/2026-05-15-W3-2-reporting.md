## Hooks for downstream work

- The reporting service is read-only and additive. New reports require
  one new method on `IReportingService` + one new entry in
  `app/pages/admin/reports/[slug].vue`'s dispatch table.
- CSV export composable can be reused by any list page — saved-views
  (W3-5) is a natural caller.
- The dashboard polls on date-range change only; future SSE/WebSocket
  promotion is purely additive (replace the `await reporting.dashboardKpis`
  with a subscription).
